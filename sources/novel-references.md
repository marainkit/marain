# Marain in the Culture novels

A curated index of what the novels establish about Marain. Each entry paraphrases the passage and quotes only a short phrase where the wording matters. Look the passage up in your own copy for the full context.

Full-text extractions produced by [`../tools/scripts/rag-extract.py`](../tools/scripts/rag-extract.py) live in `sources/novel-extractions/` on your machine. They're **gitignored** and not distributed.

---

## What the novels establish

| Fact | Source | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Marain has **one personal pronoun** covering every sex, age and kind of sentient being. Sex can be specified but isn't in everyday speech. The narrator calls Marain "language-as-moral-weapon". | *The Player of Games*, start of Part Two (the narrator's "textual note") | The canonical basis for the gender-neutral pronoun and for reading Marain as values-engineered. |
| Marain is **synthetic**, designed to be as phonetically and philosophically expressive as the pan-human speech apparatus and brain allow. One Mind compares it to the game of Azad. | *The Player of Games*, the drone Flere-Imsaho's reflections | The richest description of design intent in the novels. |
| **"Nonary"** means "based on nine": "ordinary Marain", the "three-by-three dot grid" learned in kindergarten. | *Excession*, signal-sequence interlude | Source of the term *nonary Marain* (M1). |
| Signals are tagged "standard nonary Marain" and "Marain clear". | *Excession*, signal headers | Marain as a transmission format. |
| An "M32-level transmission" is mentioned. | *Excession* | The only sourced encryption tier. M8/M16 tiers sometimes cited online are **not** found in the extracted text. |
| Marain is spoken over a tight-beam communicator. | *Consider Phlebas* | Transmission use, spoken register. |
| Culture craft carry Marain writing; outsiders' Marain is described as "crude" or "shaky". | *Consider Phlebas* | Marain as the galactic lingua franca. |
| Marain is described as "complexly simple", and some expressions resist translation into English "without at least a three-dimensional diagram". | *The State of the Art* | Texture only, no technical content. |
| Sma's poem is credited "Marain original, own translation". | *Use of Weapons*, epigraph | Marain as a literary language. |
| *Inversions* never mentions Marain. | — | The Culture presence there is covert. |

**What the novels don't establish:** glyph values, grammar, tones, or any reference to damask weaving or textiles carrying Marain. Treat claims along those lines as project inference unless a passage is found.

## Ship names as a corpus

Ship names (*Excession* alone has 100+) are the largest body of Culture naming conventions. The extraction script collects them per book, and they're useful for vocabulary and register work. Ship names are short titles and can be cited freely.
